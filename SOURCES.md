# Sources and attribution

## Seminar materials

The presentation is prepared for a seminar by Luis P. de la Horra, Universidad de Valladolid. The LaTeX bibliography lists the original studies cited in the slides.

The seminar draws on Scott Cunningham's [*Causal Inference: The Remix*](https://mixtape.scunning.com/), especially [Chapter 6, Regression Discontinuity](https://mixtape.scunning.com/06-regression_discontinuity), and the fuzzy RD discussion in Chapter 7.

## Figures

The original textbook figure URLs are recorded in [presentation/assets/sources.json](presentation/assets/sources.json). Where SVG and PDF files share a stem, the PDF is a format conversion for use with LaTeX.

The continuity schematic `smoothness_observed_counterfactual.pdf` is an excerpt of page 3 of the supplied seminar handout `rdd-smoothness-and-hoekstra.pdf`. Its source entry records that provenance; the full handout is not required to compile the slides. The hypothetical dollar amounts in the university example illustrate continuity and are not estimates from Hoekstra (2009).

The graphs in `presentation/practical/hansen_results/` were generated by the seminar exercise from the teaching dataset. The CSV tables contain the saved exercise estimates. The LaTeX table fragments are generated from those CSV files by `build_tables.py`.

## Hansen exercise and data

The exercise adapts the Hansen example and code accompanying Cunningham's RD chapter. It studies the 0.08 BAC threshold using Cunningham's publicly available [214,558-observation teaching extract](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scunning1975/causal-inference-class/master/hansen_dwi.dta).

The original study is Benjamin Hansen (2015), [“Punishment and Deterrence: Evidence from Drunk Driving,” *American Economic Review* 105(4), 1581–1617](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20130189). The seminar exercise is not a full replication of that paper. Its dataset is downloaded from the original teaching source by the do-file and is not redistributed in this repository.
